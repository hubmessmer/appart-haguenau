06Recycling and eco-friendly habits
  • Waste sorting is in place, with the instructions posted in the kitchen. Under the sink, the front bin is for general waste, the back one for recyclable packaging, and the small green caddy for food waste.
  • Organic toiletries and cleaning products, in small sizes.
  • Turn off the heating or air conditioning when the windows are open or when you are out.
  • Short showers, full loads in the dishwasher and washing machine: small things that make a difference.
  • The town centre is an 800 m walk away, Strasbourg is a direct TER train ride, and the Haguenau forest is best explored by bike.

07Neighbourhood and local tips

Haguenau, the capital of Northern Alsace, has a lively historic centre 800 m away. Strasbourg is about 40 min by car, or 21 to 30 min by direct TER train. The apartment occupies the ground floor of a renovated 1930s house with external insulation.
