The apartment

77 m², 2 real bedrooms, private parking, rare in Haguenau

Located on the ground floor of a renovated 1930s house with external thermal insulation in Haguenau (Alsace), this 77 m² apartment offers 2 separate bedrooms, a queen-size bed and two single beds, for up to 4 guests. Scandinavian style, bright, quiet.

The apartment is designed for fully autonomous use: smart lock with personal code per booking, motorised gate. The host lives on-site and remains reachable if needed.

Room by room

  • Equipped kitchen: opening onto a sunny balcony with garden furniture.
  • Bedroom 1: queen-size bed 160 × 200, wardrobe with mirrored doors and a desk for working in peace.
  • Bedroom 2: two single beds 90 × 200, wardrobe.
  • Bathroom: walk-in shower, toilet, heated towel rail.
  • Entrance: bench and slippers for the whole family.

Who is it for?

Tourism, family, weekend getaway or business trip

The apartment welcomes families on an Alsatian getaway, couples on a weekend break, groups of friends, and business travellers alike. With 77 m², 2 separate bedrooms and private parking, it's the perfect base for exploring Northern Alsace.

Tourism: Haguenau is 30 min from Strasbourg and close to the Vosges du Nord regional nature park. The Haguenau Forest (13,400 ha), one of the largest lowland forests in France, holds the Forêt d'Exception® certification since 2020, renewed until 2031. Accessible on foot from the neighbourhood.

During Advent: the Haguenau Christmas market sets up some sixty wooden chalets in the town centre, an 800 m walk away, with a vintage carousel, mulled wine and dampfnudel. Strasbourg, the “Capital of Christmas”, whose Christkindelsmärik dates back to 1570, is 21 to 30 min away by direct train.

→ Haguenau Tourist Office
→ Alsace Verte Tourist Office (Outre-Forêt)

🐇 The people of Haguenau have a nickname : the Sandhaas, "sand hares" in Alsatian dialect, after the many hares that roamed the sandy forest. The Sandhaas is now the official mascot of the city.

Business travellers: Haguenau is a major industrial hub in Northern Alsace: Siemens, Schaeffler, SEW-Usocome and Trumpf all have sites here, and CAF builds its trains in Reichshoffen, about twenty kilometres away. The apartment is ideal for managers and engineers on assignment: 1 Gb/s fibre, a desk, self check-in, private parking, 2 separate bedrooms for colleagues travelling together.

Location

Haguenau, Alsace, ideally located

Haguenau is the 2nd city of Bas-Rhin, 30 km north of Strasbourg. A key road (A4/A35) and rail hub, it hosts a dense industrial fabric: its urban area has 26 business parks covering more than 900 hectares.

Accessibility

Single-level apartment, reached by a few steps

The apartment is on the ground floor and all its rooms are on one level, with a walk-in shower. It is reached from outside by a few steps. The private parking space is right next to the house. The apartment is not suitable for wheelchairs. If you have any question about access, please write to me before booking: I will be happy to help.
